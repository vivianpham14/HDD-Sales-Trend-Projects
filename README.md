# Ducky Co. Hard Drives - Revenue Key Findings

## Project Background
Hard drives (HDD) have continued to maintain their prevalence as companies look towards investing in AI and data inferencing. With this, there is a significant increase in demand for high-capacity data storage option. 

<br>

A new company, Ducky Co. Hard Drives, concluded their first year in business and wants to better understand their revenue metrics, key customer segments, areas of interest, popular products, and recommendations moving forward.

<br>

Insights and recommendations are summarized below with the focus on key analyses, such as:
1. Sales Trend Analysis by Quarter in FY24
2. Sales Trend Analysis by City
3. Customer Segment Information
4. Popular Products

<br>

> [!NOTE]
> This personal project was created using synthetic data to mirror what has been observed in companies within the industry, adding another layer of complexity and data cleaning.

## Tools & Technologies
Synthetic data was created using ChatGPT and generated using Python code. Data cleaning and validation was performed prior to any analyses using Microsoft Excel and MySQL Workbench. Tableau was utilized for dashboard visualization of key findings and analysis. Refer to the process documented in detail at [*datanotes.md*](datanotes.md)

## Executive Summary
Ducky Co. Hard Drive’s concluded FY24 with a total of 5,000 orders approximating $2.7M in sales. The company’s sales were primarily driven by enterprise customers and mostly comprised of one-time purchases, approximately 97%. As this is the company’s first year in business, the following sections will explore the data with added context and highlight opportunities for improvement.
