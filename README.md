## Project Background
Hard drives (HDD) have continued to maintain their prevalence as companies look towards investing in AI and data inferencing. With this, there is a significant increase in demand for high-capacity data storage option. 

<br>

A new HDD company, Ducky Co. Hard Drives, concluded their first year in business and wants to better understand their revenue metrics, key customer segments, areas of interest, popular products, and recommendations moving forward. The company has entered the market with 5 HDD product lines: Internal, NAS, Enterprise, Surveillance, and Portable.

<br>

Insights and recommendations are summarized below with the focus on key analyses, such as:
1. Sales Trend Analysis by Quarter in FY24
2. Sales Trend Analysis by City
3. Customer Segment Information
4. Popular Products

## Executive Summary
Ducky Co. Hard Drive’s concluded FY24 with a total of 5,000 orders approximating $2.7M in sales. 50% of the company’s sales were primarily driven by enterprise customers and mostly comprised of one-time purchases. As this is the company’s first year in business, the following sections will explore the data with added context and highlight opportunities for improvement.

***Sales Trend Overview***
1. QoQ sales fluctuated due to seasonality with the company's highest rate of growth from Q3 to Q4.
2. The Enterprise customer segment is the largest source of sales, as large companies continue to hyperscale. This trend is expected to continue moving forward.
3. Highest number of shipments made to enterprise customers located in Sacramento, Seattle, and Chicago.
4. 97% of sales transactions recorded were one-time purchases from different customers.

***Product Performance***

3. No recorded sales from the Portable product line.

## Recommendations
Based on the key findings section and upon further research on next steps, recommendations are presented below to strengthen business and financial operations as the company continues forward-looking efforts.

⭐ ***Discontinue production for the next year and attempt to sell-through all Portable and Surveillance product lines at a discounted price or through bundle deals with other purchases.*** 
  - Surveillance and Portable product lines account for <2% of total sales for the year. The plan is to slightly offset and recover from the losses.
  - Reassess whether the company should discontinue the product lines or slowly reintegrate production.

<br>

⭐ ***Build and maintain customer relationships with customers to increase customer retention and reduce one-time purchases.***
  - Customer Service Lines & Emails: ensure fast response times to customer inquiries with excellent communication and always have a call to action (e.g., have associates with technical knowledge of the products, where to refer for more resources, who to direct call to if a specialist is needed, etc.)
  - Provide sales discounts or rebates as an incentive for customer loyalty
    - Enterprise & Small Businesses aiming to scale (requires contract negotiation):
      - Tier-pricing model: adjustment in price following the purchase of a specific number of hard drives
      - Competitive pricing: match competitor prices based on the agreed contract price
      - Offer rebates for specific product offerings based on TB
    - Consumers & Small Businesses
      - 10% discount for ad and newsletter subscription
      - Customize and cater advertisements based on previous purchases
      - Holiday discounts to drive Q4 sales
      - Competitor price match based on retail postings

<br>

⭐ ***Invest more R&D into NAS product line, 8TB Enterprise HDD, and 2TB Internal HDD and increase profit margin.***
  - Advertise to increase sales of the company's best-selling products and share customer testimonials.
  - Find ways to reduce cost without compromising quality -- negotiate with suppliers and manufacturers to secure fixed rates.
  - Reach out to third-party investors to raise additional capital for R&D.

<br>

⭐ ***Continue to monitor the ship-to areas for the next 2-3 years.***
  - If the same ship-to-city are consistently purchasing more, consider investing in a warehouse facility near the area to reduce shipping costs.
